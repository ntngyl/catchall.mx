Email forwarding for a domain on OVHcloud

Send every address at your domain to the inbox you already use. It takes two DNS records at OVHcloud and about two minutes. Free, and there is no account to create.

Before you start

  • The domain's DNS is at OVHcloud. Its nameservers end in ovh.net, ovh.ca or anycast.me. If you are not sure, the record builder looks it up and detects the provider for you.
  • You know where the mail should go. Any inbox you already read: Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, a work address.
  • Nothing else needs this domain's mail. Forwarding replaces whatever receives it now. Mailboxes at Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or your host stop receiving once the MX record changes.
Type your inbox address here and the records below are filled in for you. It stays in your browser.

The stepsWhat to click and type at OVHcloud.

  1. Open the DNS records.

    Log in to the OVHcloud Manager and open Web Cloud. Go to Domains & DNS, then Portfolio, and choose your domain. Open the DNS zone tab and click Create a record for each record below.

  2. Delete the MX records that are already there.

    A domain should have one mail service. If old MX records stay, some mail keeps going to the old place. Leave every other record alone; your website is not affected.

  3. Add the MX record.

    This tells the world that catchall.mx receives the domain's mail. Subdomain is @, which means the domain itself.

    TypeMX
    Subdomain@
    Priority10
    Targetmail.catchall.mx
  4. Add the TXT record.

    This tells us where to send the mail. Use the address where you actually read email.

    TypeTXT
    Subdomain@
    Valuecatchall-forward=you@example.com
  5. Check it and confirm once.

    Enter the domain below. When both records are right, a button appears that emails a confirmation link to your inbox. Click the link and forwarding starts. The confirmation exists so nobody can point a domain at your address and flood you.

Things to know at OVHcloud

  • The form adds the final dot to the mail server name itself; type it without one.
  • The normal form limits a TXT value to 255 characters. That is plenty for these records, but a DKIM key is longer: if you add DKIM later, use Modify zone (text mode) for that one record.

Menu names were checked against OVHcloud's own help pages in November 2026. If a screen looks different, the record builder reads your domain's current records and tells you exactly what to add, change or delete.

If something is off

The checker still shows the old records

DNS changes are usually visible within a few minutes, but an old answer can be cached for as long as the record's previous TTL, often an hour. Wait and check again before changing anything.

The confirmation email did not arrive

Look in the spam folder first. The link is valid for 24 hours, and the checker can send it again after ten minutes.

Mail from some senders is missing

Look for leftover MX records from a previous mail service; the MX lookup shows all of them. Mail that arrives before you confirm is held for 24 hours and delivered once you do.

You want to stop

Delete the two records. Nothing else needs undoing, and there is no account to close.

After it worksOptional extras.

Different addresses to different inboxes

More TXT records can send single addresses elsewhere, block addresses that get spam, or accept only a list you choose. The record builder writes them for you.

Sign forwarded mail as your domain

Adding one DKIM record lets forwarded mail be signed by your own domain, which helps it reach the inbox. It is optional, and the builder covers it too.

Check the rest of your email DNS

The DNS check looks at MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC together.
