Somewhere else?The general version.

Any provider that lets you add MX and TXT records works. The records are:

MXmail.catchall.mx  priority 10
TXTcatchall-forward=you@example.com

Both go on the domain itself, which most providers write as @. The record builder reads what your domain has now and lists what to add, change or delete. It also knows DigitalOcean, Gandi and Name.com.

catchall.mx is a small, free email forwarder.
