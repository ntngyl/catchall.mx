CatchAll.mx and Forward Email compared

Forward Email is the closest thing to CatchAll.mx: its free plan is also configured with DNS TXT records. It does far more on top, including sending, mailboxes, regular expressions and webhooks, with paid plans from $3 a month.

Which one fitsAn honest read.

Forward Email is the better choice if

  • You want to send as your domain or have real mailboxes later, without changing provider.
  • You need regular expressions, several recipients for one address, or webhooks.
  • You want your forwarding addresses hidden from public DNS (their paid plans do this; neither free option does).
  • You receive large attachments.

CatchAll.mx is the better choice if

  • You want forwarding only, with nothing to sign up for and no plan to outgrow.
  • You want the mailbox owner to confirm before any mail is sent to them.
  • You prefer plain rules (catchall-route, catchall-block, catchall-only) over regular expressions.
  • You want a permissive licence (MIT) on the code.

Moving from Forward Email

  1. Delete the two Forward Email MX records (mx1.forwardemail.net and mx2.forwardemail.net) and the forward-email= TXT records.
  2. Add an MX record for mail.catchall.mx with priority 10, and a TXT record catchall-forward=you@example.com. The record builder reads your domain and lists exactly what to add and delete.
  3. Check the domain on the homepage and send the confirmation email to your inbox.

Mail sent during the switch is not lost: sending servers retry for days, and mail that arrives before you confirm waits up to 24 hours.
