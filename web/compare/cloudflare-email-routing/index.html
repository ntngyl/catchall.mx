CatchAll.mx and Cloudflare Email Routing compared

If your domain already uses Cloudflare for DNS, Cloudflare Email Routing is free, has no stated inbound limit and takes slightly larger messages. It only works for domains on Cloudflare DNS, and it is managed from a Cloudflare account. CatchAll.mx works with any DNS provider and needs no account.

Which one fitsAn honest read.

Cloudflare is the better choice if

  • Your domain is already on Cloudflare DNS and you are happy to manage mail there.
  • You need more than a few dozen emails a day.
  • You want to process mail in code with Email Workers.
  • You want the backing of a large network over a small independent service.

CatchAll.mx is the better choice if

  • Your DNS is somewhere else and you do not want to move nameservers just to forward mail.
  • You want the same setup to work on every domain you own, whichever provider each one uses.
  • You do not want mail routing tied to an account.
  • You want mail from senders with weak authentication forwarded with a safe From line; Cloudflare rejects mail that fails both SPF and DKIM.

Moving from Cloudflare

  1. Turn off Email Routing for the domain in the Cloudflare dashboard so its MX records are removed, then add the two CatchAll.mx records. Our Cloudflare guide has the exact fields.
  2. Add an MX record for mail.catchall.mx with priority 10, and a TXT record catchall-forward=you@example.com. The record builder reads your domain and lists exactly what to add and delete.
  3. Check the domain on the homepage and send the confirmation email to your inbox.

Mail sent during the switch is not lost: sending servers retry for days, and mail that arrives before you confirm waits up to 24 hours.
