The short version

  • You want to send mail as your domain too: ImprovMX or Forward Email, on a paid plan.
  • Your DNS is already at Cloudflare and you want no limits to think about: Cloudflare Email Routing.
  • You want forwarding and nothing else, with no account, on any DNS provider: CatchAll.mx.

One by one

  • CatchAll.mx and ImprovMXImprovMX is a polished, long-running forwarder with a dashboard, an API and paid plans that add sending.→
