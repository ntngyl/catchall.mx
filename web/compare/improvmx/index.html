CatchAll.mx and ImprovMX compared

ImprovMX is a polished, long-running forwarder with a dashboard, an API and paid plans that add sending. CatchAll.mx is smaller and plainer: no account, no paid tier, and every setting is a DNS record.

Which one fitsAn honest read.

ImprovMX is the better choice if

  • You want to send mail as your domain, not only receive it.
  • You need attachments over 20 MB, more than 50 emails a day without touching DNS, or a delivery log to look at.
  • You want an API, webhooks, or support you can write to.
  • You would rather not have your destination address visible in DNS.

CatchAll.mx is the better choice if

  • You have several domains and do not want to pay to forward more than one.
  • You do not want another account and password for something this small.
  • You want settings you can read, copy and version alongside the rest of your DNS.
  • You want to be able to read the code that handles your mail.

Moving from ImprovMX

  1. Delete the two ImprovMX MX records (mx1.improvmx.com and mx2.improvmx.com). The SPF record that includes spf.improvmx.com is not needed for CatchAll.mx; remove that include unless you send through ImprovMX.
  2. Add an MX record for mail.catchall.mx with priority 10, and a TXT record catchall-forward=you@example.com. The record builder reads your domain and lists exactly what to add and delete.
  3. Check the domain on the homepage and send the confirmation email to your inbox.

Mail sent during the switch is not lost: sending servers retry for days, and mail that arrives before you confirm waits up to 24 hours.
