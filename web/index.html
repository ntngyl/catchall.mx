CatchAll.mx

Simple Email Forwarding for Developers

2 DNS records. Done. All emails to *@yourdomain.com forward to you.

How It Works

1

Add MX Record

Point your domain's MX record to our server:

mail.catchall.mx (priority 10)
2

Add TXT Record

Set where emails should forward to:

catchall-forward=you@gmail.com
3

Verify & Done

Send a test email, click verification link, and you're all set! All emails to *@yourdomain.com will forward to you.

⚠️ Important: Check Your Spam Folder

📬 Verification Email

When you first send an email to your domain, we'll send you a verification email to prevent spam.

  • Check your spam/junk folder if you don't see it in your inbox
  • The subject will be: "Confirm email forwarding from yourdomain.com"
  • Mark it as "Not Spam" or move it to your inbox to ensure future emails are delivered properly

🔗 Forwarded Emails with URLs

Emails containing links may sometimes be filtered as spam by your email provider.

  • Always check your spam folder if you're expecting an email
  • Mark legitimate emails as "Not Spam" to train your email provider
  • Add forwarded@catchall.mx to your contacts/safe senders list

✅ How to Prevent Spam Filtering

To ensure all forwarded emails reach your inbox:

  • Whitelist verify@catchall.mx and forwarded@catchall.mx
  • When you receive the verification email, mark it as "Not Spam" immediately
  • Move verification and forwarded emails to your inbox folder
  • This helps Gmail, Outlook, and other providers recognize our emails as legitimate
