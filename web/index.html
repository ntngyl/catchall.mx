Setup

Two records, then one click.

Add these at your DNS provider, on the domain itself (host @). Your DNS is the control panel.

01

Point your mail to us

An MX record tells senders where to deliver mail for your domain.

MX · priority 10
mail.catchall.mx
02

Say where it should go

A TXT record names the mailbox that receives everything.

TXT
catchall-forward=you@example.com
03

Confirm once

Check your domain above and press "Send confirmation email". Your mailbox gets a link to click, so nobody can forward mail to an address that did not agree.

No link in your inbox? Check the spam folder and mark it "Not spam".

Rules

Control each address, still without an account.

Optional TXT records decide what happens to individual addresses. Use the part before the @, and separate several entries with commas. Prefer not to write them by hand? The record builder writes them for you.

Route

Send one address to a different mailbox. That mailbox confirms once before it receives anything.

TXT
catchall-route=billing:accounts@example.com

Block

Refuse an address that started attracting junk. * matches anything, so news-* covers a whole family.

TXT
catchall-block=oldshop,news-*

Allow-list

Accept only the addresses you name and refuse the rest. Routed addresses are always accepted.

TXT
catchall-only=info,sales,me

Privacy

Your mail passes through. It does not stay.

There are no accounts, so there is no profile of you to keep. This is everything that happens to a message.

01

Arrives

We read your domain's DNS records to learn where it goes. Blocked addresses are refused before the message is even sent.

02

Checked

Spam and forged senders are filtered on our own server. Message content is never sent to a third party.

03

Forwarded

Delivered to your mailbox over an encrypted connection where your provider supports it, and signed so it passes authentication.

04

Gone

Once your provider accepts the message, our copy is deleted. There is no archive and no backup of mail.

What we keep

  • Your domain's status: that it is confirmed, with each confirmed destination stored as a fingerprint.
  • Daily counters per domain, to enforce limits.
  • Delivery logs for 7 days: domain, destination and time, for troubleshooting. Then they are deleted.
  • Mail on hold: messages waiting for a destination to confirm are kept 24 hours at most. Messages held as suspected forgeries are kept 7 days at most. Then they are deleted.

What we never do

  • No reading or mining of message content.
  • No tracking pixels or link rewriting in your mail.
  • No selling or sharing of addresses or metadata.
  • No lock-in: remove the two DNS records and we are out of the path.
Questions

Good to know before you switch.

Is it really free?

Yes, and it will stay free. There is no paid tier and no account to upgrade. Daily limits exist only to keep the service healthy for everyone.

Why does some forwarded mail say "via CatchAll" in the sender name?

Mail providers reject forwarded messages that cannot prove where they came from. When the original sender signed their message, we forward it untouched. When they did not, we send it from our own address, keep the real sender in the name and in Reply-To, and sign it, so it reaches your inbox and your replies still go to the right person.

The confirmation email or a forwarded message went to spam. What now?

Mark it "Not spam" once. That is the strongest signal your provider takes, and later mail usually follows it to the inbox. Adding forwarded@catchall.mx and verify@catchall.mx to your contacts helps too.

What happens if I change the forwarding address?

The new mailbox receives a confirmation link the next time mail arrives, and forwarding resumes as soon as it is clicked. Mail that arrives in between is held for it.

Can I send or reply from my domain?

CatchAll.mx is receive-only. Replies leave from your own mailbox. Most mail providers let you add your domain address as a "send as" identity if you have an outgoing mail server for it.

Does my domain need to be new or unused?

No, but mail for a domain is delivered to one place. If the domain already has mailboxes elsewhere, pointing its MX record here moves all of its incoming mail to forwarding.

Someone is abusing the service. How do I report it?

Use the abuse report form. Domains and destinations used for abuse are blocked.
